# Assassin (Sát thủ): mechanics and behaviour

Two uses of one unit: the **enemy assassin** a village sends against one villager (`sim.sendAssassin`), and the **specialist soldier** the player trains in the guard camp (`specialists.json` units, id `assassin`). Code: `src/sim/specialists.ts` (state machine), `src/sim/brainfoe.ts` (duel), `src/sim/battle.ts` (dodge), `src/sim/movement.ts` (poses). Numbers: `src/data/specialists.json` (`assassin` block = enemy behaviour, `units[id=assassin]` = trained soldier).

## Enemy assassin: state machine

| State | What he does | Leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| `stalk` | Hidden. Walks a wide way round to a point behind the target (4 tiles behind while far, 1 tile when close). If 3+ of our soldiers are within 3 tiles of the target he waits 6 tiles away in the shadows | target within `dashRange` (1.5): start a pass. Target dead or 150 s gone: `flee` |
| `pass` | Dash in a straight line **through** the target and 1.5 tiles beyond, speed x5, drawn faint. One cut as he goes through | path ends: second pass (`stalk`, 0.1 s pause), or `duel` after 2 passes, or `flee` if the target is dead |
| `duel` | Stays and fights like a normal soldier (`battle.engage`, combo), in plain sight | target dead: `flee`. Weak soldier: finishing pass (below) |
| `flee` | Runs to his camp, then vanishes in smoke (0.75 s) and is removed | gone |

- **Pass damage:** pass 1 = `strikeMult` x target max health (deadly, before armour, bodyguard share applies); pass 2 = same x `followMult` (0.6).
- **Only the death of the assigned target makes him run.** Losing a duel round, being hit, or taking blows never makes him retreat.
- **Finishing pass:** in `duel`, every 0.3 s he looks for one of our units within 1.5 tiles with health below 5% that is fighting him (or is the target). The next dash goes through that unit and kills it (damage x10 max health). It does not count as one of the 2 passes. Then he returns to the duel.
- **Hidden:** unseen by us until a soldier is within 3 tiles, a tower within 4.5, or after he goes in (then `engaged`, always seen). A revealing event lasts 8 s.
- **Target choice** (`pickTarget`): a villager with a bodyguard first, else a non-soldier, else anyone.

## Config (`specialists.json` > `assassin`)

| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `strikeMult`, `followMult` | pass 1 damage (x max health), pass 2 factor |
| `passes`, `passBeyond`, `passMul`, `passPause` | number of passes, tiles run past the target, speed factor while dashing, pause between passes |
| `dashRange`, `backDist`, `lurkDist` | when the dash starts, how far behind the target he aims, how close he comes to a guarded target |
| `crowd`, `crowdRadius` | soldiers needed round the target to keep him off, and their radius |
| `executeBelow`, `executeRange` | finishing pass: health fraction and search range (1.5 tiles) |
| `revealRadius`, `towerRadius`, `revealSeconds` | how he is spotted |
| `spawnDist`, `fleeSeconds`, `giveUpSeconds`, `vanishSeconds`, `hpMul`, `speedMul` | spawn distance from the fire, run limit, hunt limit, smoke time, health and speed factors |

## Poses used by the game (`movement.animate`)

`dagger_vanish` (vanishing) > `dagger_dodge` (just slipped a blow) > `dagger_stab` (strike pose, flee start) > `dagger_dash` (pass) or `dagger_run` (moving as the hired killer) > normal style poses (`dagger_guard`, `dagger_combo` when fighting, `dagger_idle`, `dagger_walk`).

## Crossing fences and walls (`vault`)

Units whose specialist has `"vault": true` (the assassin, trained or enemy) treat every fence of ours that stands as ground they can cross. Code: `walltop.ts` (`vaultCosts`, `vaultAt`, `vaultPose`, `vaultSpeed`), `path.ts` (`leap` argument), `movement.ts`. Numbers: `data/walls.json` > `vault`.

| Fence | Action | Path cost | Speed | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| level below 6 (`minLevel`) | somersault over | 0.5 | x0.7 | `dagger_leap` (tuck and tumble, `rot` field in the rig) |
| level 6 and up (high wall) | climb over | 1.5 | x0.45 | `dagger_climb` |

- The path search goes through a fence only when it saves more than its cost, so he still prefers an open way or a gate when it is not much longer.
- Gates, towers and unfinished fences are not crossed. Player orders use the same path (`pathTo`), so ordering him across a fence works.
- The pose shows from a little before the fence until he is off it.
